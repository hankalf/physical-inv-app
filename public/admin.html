Auto-assign the aisles a staggered plan for every team, in one go

The aisles are walked in order and split into one continuous stretch per team, about the same racking each, so the teams start spread across the building and never bunch up. A team gets only the levels its equipment reaches; what it cannot reach goes to the nearest team that can, after its own stretch. Preview shows the plan before anything is queued.

Team assignments

Queue aisles per team in counting order, with the levels that team's equipment covers (A–C on foot, D–F with the lift, A–F for all). Levels are required. Two teams can share an aisle on different levels. A team's next aisle waits while another team holds those levels in its racking block — that is what staggers them. Which aisles share racking is set under Settings.

SOS from the floor raised on a scanner · newest first

A counter presses one button and picks what is wrong. It lands here, and in your Teams channel if one is set up (Settings → Advanced). I am on it tells the scanner somebody has seen it — which is what the person who pressed it is waiting for. Close ends it, with a note of what happened.

Message the floor a line on the scanners of one team, or all of them

It lands on the handheld within about twenty seconds and stays on the screen until a counter taps Got it — so this shows who has actually read it, not who was told.

Second counts

A second count is a bin somebody goes back to. They are raised automatically when a first count disagrees with the inventory report (quantity differs, wrong bin, not on the report, counted twice), when an aisle is handed back with expected pallets nobody saw, or by hand here. The counter is told the bin and the reason type, never the numbers, and the team that did the first count is never offered the second. Lines from a second count replace the first for that bin.

Adjustments what this count would do to the ERP

Every pallet that differs from the inventory report, biggest first. Approve one with a reason code and it goes into the ERP file; reject it and the system keeps its number. Nothing leaves in the Adjustments export until it has been signed for — and a line counted again after it was approved comes back here, because the number somebody signed for is no longer the number.
Approvals are off for this count.
Turn on Adjustments need approval under Settings → Getting started → Count session to use this. With it off, every variance goes into the ERP file as it always did.

Fix list what the floor found that somebody has to put right

A count walks every bin in the building. What the counters see on the way is written down here instead of lost on the radio: labels that would not scan (pallet or rack — rack ones first, every counter after walks up to that bin too), damage reported from the gun (a pallet, the racking, the product), and bins blocked by a trailer or a forklift that a team said it would come back to. Tick each one off as it is dealt with.

Find a lot after a recall notice: where is every case of it

Searches what was actually counted, and what the inventory report expected — so it answers from the floor, not from what the ERP believed before the count started. Needs the lot code question turned on, or a report that carries lot codes.
