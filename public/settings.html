      Scanner setup each scanner gets its own link — open it once on the device and add it to the home screen

      On the device: open the link in Chrome (or scan the QR into the address bar), then menu → Add to Home screen. Opening the link signs that scanner in — the server accepts counts from signed-in scanners only. Treat a link like a key: Reset link issues a new one and kills the old, and removing a scanner stops it entirely. A link used more than once is flagged, which is normal after a scanner is wiped and worth a look otherwise.

      What the scanners offer the one-tap reasons a counter can pick, without typing

      The reasons a site needs are the site's own — "Blocked by a trailer" means something here and nothing anywhere else. A scanner picks up a change within about half a minute, without anybody signing out of it.

      Comments

      Offered on the last step, after the bin is scanned.

      Override reasons

      Offered when a pallet ID is not on the list and the counter is allowed to accept it anyway.
      How long the comments step waits before moving on by itself is set on the Scanner screen tab, with the rest of what the counting screen does.

      SOS from a scanner what a counter can raise, and the channel it goes to

      Every gun has an SOS button. Pressing it offers this list, and whichever one the counter picks lands on the dashboard within seconds — with their team, aisle and last bin — and in a Teams channel if one is set up under Advanced. Write the list in the words your floor uses; the counter is in a hurry.

      When a team stops scanning

      A team that is signed on and still has an aisle, but has not scanned for this long, gets an amber bar on the dashboard — On break, Lunch or Seen quiets it. Signing off on the gun stops a team's clock. 0 turns it off.

      Bin list every location in the warehouse

      What a scanned bin is checked against, and what defines the aisles teams get assigned to. Upload this one first.
      The ERP bin export that ships with the app: racks F01–F24 and A01–A04 plus WIP, the NIL bin and other areas. Staging lanes and dock doors are counted manually and are left out.

      Inventory report what the system thinks is on hand

      What the count is compared against — it is where variances, second counts and the ERP adjustments come from. Re-upload it whenever the ERP moves on. Three systems share the warehouse: name the system each report came from (or put a System column in the file) and the office side keeps them apart — the pallet report, adjustments and one ERP file per system. The scanners see no difference.

      Aisles & racking blocks

      Aisles in the same block share racking, so only one team can be active in a block at a time. Pair them automatically, or type a block name on any row. Teams are queued onto these aisles on the Dashboard.

      Supervisor logins
        everyone who changes something is recorded by name in the log
      
      
      
        
      
          Give each supervisor their own login. Admins can manage accounts, Settings and everything else;
          supervisors get the pages and functions ticked under May use. Deactivating a login keeps its history
          in the log but stops it being used. The superadmin is the login the site started with, set in the server's
          environment (Railway variables); there is no shared password.
        
      
        
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
            
            
          
      
          
      
        
      
        
      
          Leave the password blank and one is generated for you — read it out once, and they choose
          their own the first time they sign in. Type one instead and they still have to replace it,
          so nobody but its owner ever knows a working password.
