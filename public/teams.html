Teams drag people between the crew list and a team — or use the menu on a card

⬇ Sample sheet
One row per seat: Team #, Department, Employee, Equipment (a Clock In Number column is used when there is one). A team number like S1-DS01 reads as 1st shift, dock stocker, truck 01; S2-… is 2nd shift. A name found on the crew list is put on the team; a blank name is an open seat — Production sends someone on the day — and its equipment still counts toward what the team can reach. Each team on the sheet gets the sheet's seats; other teams are left alone.

Counting plan which team takes which aisle, for a count

One row per aisle, in the order each team should count it, with the levels their equipment reaches. Optional — aisles can be queued by hand under Dashboard → Team plan, or spread out with Auto-assign.

Crew workbook employees and teams in one Excel file — checked against each other before anything changes

An Employees sheet (Clock In Number, Name, Department, Shift, Equipment, and sizes if you like) and a Teams sheet (Team #, Department, Employee, Equipment, Clock In Number) — or one sheet with both sets of columns, a row per person with their team, and a row with a team and equipment but no name for an open seat. Check reads it and says what does not match; Apply loads both at once when it is clean.

Add employees one at a time, or the whole crew list from a file

⬇ Sample CSV
Columns matched by name: Clock In Number (also accepts Badge, Employee ID), then Name, Department, Shift (1 or 2), Equipment, and — if you have them — Jacket, Bibs, Boots. Equipment can be a list — Scissor lift; High reach. Spellings like forklift, reach truck, pallet jack are understood.

Employees everyone on the crew list — Edit changes a person, Remove takes them off

Gear sizes jacket, bibs (overalls) and boots for everyone on the crew list

Pick a size and it is saved. The export is an Excel workbook: everyone's name, department and sizes, the grand total of each size, and the same totals by department — what to order. Sizes can also come in on the crew-list upload, in Jacket, Bibs and Boots columns.

What each level needs a team must have all of it between them

Level A is on foot. Everything above needs equipment, and a level reachable two ways lists both rules. An aisle assignment for levels a team cannot reach is refused in the dashboard.
