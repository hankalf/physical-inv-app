First time here? How this page works

This is a safe place to learn the scanner. The screen on the left is the real scanner app — the same one on the Zebra guns — counting into a practice count that belongs to you alone. The right side is the warehouse: what is on each shelf, and a checklist of things to try. The dashboard at the top is the office side of the same count. Nothing you do here touches a real count.

What to practise the full count, or the other jobs a scanner does

Before you start

Try the scanner on test data

The screen on the left is the real scanner app, counting into a practice count of its own — two short aisles of real Front Royal bins with a made-up report laid over them. Click any bin pallet or qty below to scan it into the gun, exactly as the trigger would. Nothing done here reaches a real count, the office board, or the floor’s scanners. The practice is yours: it is kept under your login, and anyone else signing in here gets their own.

1 · Sign on

Scan the team and a clock-in number, then tap Sign on & load list on the gun. Team 99 already has aisles F01 then F02. Your own clock-in number works too — T1001 and T1002 are made up, so the gun flags them as not on the crew list, which is exactly what it does with a mistyped one.

2 · Things to try

Try the features that ship turned off on your practice count only

These are off on a new count. Switch any of them on here to see what it does — on the gun and on the dashboard at the top of the page. It changes your practice count and nothing else; the gun picks it up between pallets.

Set for the whole site, so not changed here

The Teams channel (Settings → Advanced), the stopped-scanning alert (Settings → Scanners), and the scanner screen — large text, full screen, the question order (Settings → Scanner screen) — apply to every scanner, so changing them for a test would change them on the floor too.

Scanner settings in this sandbox what the gun shows and asks — changed here, for your practice only

These are the site-wide scanner settings (Settings → Scanner screen and Scanners). In here they belong to your practice count: change any of them to see what the gun does, and the real scanners never notice. The comments step is held for 5 seconds to start with, so there is time to read it. Back to the site's settings puts it all back.

Your own test pallets

Test with your own bins and pallets: a sheet with Bin, Pallet and Qty (a Note, Lot and Best Before are read if they are there; a row with a bin and no pallet is an empty bin). It is the same sheet as the barcode test book under Settings → Getting started — print the book, upload the same file here, and scan the paper. Uploading starts a new practice run on your data; the run you were on is kept below.

This is what a counter would find — the report is what the system thinks, and it is wrong in places on purpose. The gun asks for the pallet, then the quantity, then the bin. A green edge means the bin has been counted.
