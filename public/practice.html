Every login's practice who has used the Testing Suite, what they tried, and how far they got

Each login practices on a count of its own, and this is the one place they are all listed — for admins only. Export gives one workbook of everything a person did, with every sheet the real Export everything gives a count; Delete takes a person's practice away so they start fresh next time.
