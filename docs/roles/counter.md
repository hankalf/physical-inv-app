# SOP — Counter

**Role:** Counter (on the handheld)  **Login profile:** none — a counter uses a scanner, not a supervisor login  **Pages:** the scanner app only  **Functions:** counting, second counts, Front2Back, Not in Location, SOS

This procedure is for the people with the guns: what to do on the handheld on count day and
on the other days, in the order it happens. It is written to be read once and remembered, so it
is short. The rules in bold are the ones that matter.

## 1 — Three rules

- **Scan, do not type.** Type only when a label will not scan, and the gun will ask you.
- **A bin with nothing in it is scanned as EMPTY.** Never skipped.
- **SOS is for a stopped team, not a question.** For a question, ask when a supervisor passes.

## 2 — Signing on

1. Open the app from the home-screen icon. The gun shows its own name.
2. Pick the job at the top: **Full count**, **Cycle count**, **Front2Back**, **Not in Location**.
   Only the jobs that exist today are offered.
3. Full count: pick the **count** (it lands on the right one), type the **team number**, scan
   **every clock-in number** on the crew, pressing Enter after each (tap a number to remove
   it). Cycle count, Front2Back and Not in Location: **your own clock-in number alone**, no
   team.
4. **Sign on & load list**. The gun downloads the lists once and works with or without signal
   from then on.
5. Full count: the gun shows **your aisle** and the bins in it. **Start counting.**

## 3 — Counting: pallet, quantity, bin

The gun asks in order. Read the prompt at the top; it is the only thing you have to do next.

1. **Scan PALLET ID.** The gun says which pallet. It never says what is on it or how many: that
   is deliberate.
2. **Enter QUANTITY.** Cases on the pallet. A big number has to be typed twice.
3. **Scan LOT** and **enter EXPIRY** — only on counts that ask.
4. **Scan BIN LOCATION.** The rack label. The gun says where that bin is and which face.
5. **Comments**: tap a reason or type a note, or leave it and the gun moves on by itself.

The gun tells you the **next bin** in the aisle. A bin with several pallets stays until each is
scanned. When the aisle is done, **Aisle complete — next aisle** hands it back and the gun gives
you the next one. You do not wait for a supervisor.

## 4 — Empty bins

- One empty bin: **Bin is EMPTY — scan the bin.** Scan the rack label.
- A run of them: **Mark a run empty**: scan the **FIRST** empty location and the **LAST**,
  untick any that are not empty, confirm.

## 5 — When a label will not scan, or the gun says no

- **A pallet label will not scan**: can you read the number? Type it; it is still that pallet.
  If nothing is readable: **Counting it as a pallet with no label.** It is recorded under the
  bin and somebody comes to relabel it.
- **A rack label will not scan**: scan the bin before or after it and the gun suggests the
  one in between; if it cannot, the count is still kept and the bay goes on the relabel list.
- **"Pallet not on the list"**: the gun asks whether to count it anyway. If it is really there,
  yes: a supervisor adds it to the system. Pick the reason it offers.
- **"That label belongs to another pallet"**: a second label. Tell a supervisor; count it under
  the first label.
- **"That is a very large quantity"**: type it again if it is right.
- **"That date has passed"**: tell a supervisor; it is recorded.
- **Team N is counting aisle X**: another team holds that racking. Wait, or ask.
- **"F01A001 is a bin, not a pallet"**, **"P-100 is a pallet label, not a bin"**, **"That is a
  bin, not a quantity"**: the wrong label went into that step. Nothing was recorded. Scan what
  the prompt asks for; the quantity is always typed.

## 6 — Second counts

A bin the office wants counted again. **Start second counts** on the assignment screen gives
you the nearest ones. The gun shows the bin and why, never the numbers and never who counted
it first. Count the bin fully, then **Bin done — nothing more here**.

## 7 — Blocked, broken, help

- **Cannot reach the bins — blocked**: a trailer, a forklift. Tap it; the office knows; come
  back later.
- **Report a problem**: damage, a leak, unsafe racking, with a reason and a note.
- **SOS — I need help**: equipment broke down, you need a supervisor, someone is hurt or
  trapped. Pick the reason. *Sent* means the office has it; the gun tells you when they
  have seen it. *No signal — this has NOT been sent*: walk ten metres, it sends itself.

## 8 — The other jobs: cycle count, Front2Back, Not in Location

All three: your badge alone at sign-on.

- **Cycle count**: **My list** → **Start counting the list**. Every pallet in each bin, then
  **Bin done — nothing more here**. Each bin you count is *the* count.
- **Front2Back**: pick the **aisle**. The gun shows the **pallet**, the bin it is in (**from**)
  and the bin it goes to (**to**), with the **pallet system** underneath. Move the pallet,
  book it in that screen, tap **Moved — next**. Nothing is scanned in the app. **‹ ›** step
  through; **Aisles** goes back. A pallet that cannot be moved: **›** past it and tell the
  office.
- **Not in Location**: pick the **aisle** the pallets were last seen in. The gun shows the
  **pallet**, *last seen in* its bin, what it is, and the pallet system underneath. Found it:
  book it there, tap **Found — next**. **›** past one that is not there.

Scanning a pallet from the Not in Location list during any job makes the gun say **found!**:
count it as normal, the office is told where it turned up.

## 9 — Offline, the keyboard, the language, the screen

- **OFFLINE** with a number: your lines are queued and upload with signal. **Never clear the
  app or reinstall it** to fix it. Do not wipe the device.
- The **Keyboard** button is for typing a quantity. If a keypad covers the screen, tap it again.
- **Español / English** on the sign-on screen; the choice stays on that scanner.
- The screen keeps itself upright. If scans land in the address bar, the app is not installed:
  tell the office; the red **Tap here to scan** bar puts it right meanwhile.
- The green **Update ready** bar: tap it between pallets.

## 10 — Signing off

**Sign off this scanner** when you stop for the day, then the cradle. A gun signed off with
signal has nothing left on it; a gun that says OFFLINE goes somewhere with signal before the
cradle.

## 11 — What the gun says, and what it means

| The gun says | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| *Offline and no list cached for this session* | It has never downloaded this count | Into Wi-Fi once, sign on again |
| *this scanner is no longer authorised* | Its link was reset | A supervisor opens its new link on it |
| *Add at least one clock in number* | Sign on with no badge | Scan a badge |
| *Team N is counting aisle X* | Another team holds that racking | Wait, or ask |
| *That bin was taken by another team* | Two teams reached one bin | Move on; the gun shows the next |
| *EMPTY bin — scan its LOCATION* | Waiting for the rack label | Scan the bin, not a pallet |
| *Bin done — nothing more here* | The bin is finished | Normal: the next bin comes up |
| *F03-118 was on the Not in Location list — found!* | The office was looking for it | Count it as normal |
| *No signal — this has NOT been sent* | Your SOS or line is waiting for signal | Walk ten metres |
| *Pallet system: …* under a desk | The screen framed under the strip | Book the move or the find there |
| anything else | — | Ask a supervisor, or paste it into the User guide's Ask box |
