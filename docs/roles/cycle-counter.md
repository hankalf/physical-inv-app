# SOP — Cycle Counter

**Role:** Cycle counter  **Login profile:** *Cycle counter — cycle counts only*  **Pages:** Cycle counts, Testing Suite, User guide  **Functions:** none beyond reading

This procedure is for the person who counts a handful of bins every day as part of the
cycle programme: the gun in the morning, the Cycle counts page to see what is done and what
is left. Building the programme, uploading its lists and reading the variances are the
warehouse-jobs and inventory-control procedures.

## 1 — What a cycle count is

Instead of stopping the warehouse, a few bins are counted every day: the ones counted
longest ago first, or the ones never counted, or a sample. Each bin you count is *the* count
for that bin: what you find is what the system is compared with. There is no second opinion
unless the office raises a second count. Count as if the auditor were behind you.

## 2 — The morning: sign on and count the list

1. Pick the gun up; open the app from the home-screen icon. The sign-on screen names the
   scanner.
2. Tap **Cycle count** at the top. If it is not offered, there is no batch today, or the office
   has the job switched off for count day; ask.
3. **Scan your clock-in number** (type it and press Enter if the badge will not scan). Just
   you: a cycle count is one person with a gun, no team number.
4. **Sign on & load list**. The gun downloads the bin list once and then works with or without
   signal.
5. **My list** shows today's bins; **Start counting the list** takes you to the first. Several
   people can be on the same batch: each takes their own bins, and a bin somebody else has
   taken is not offered to you.
6. Work the list. When it is empty, **Sign off this scanner** and put the gun back on charge.

## 3 — Counting a bin properly

The gun walks you through each bin. In each one:

- **Every pallet in the bin**: scan the pallet, enter the quantity (big numbers twice),
  scan the lot and type the expiry only if the gun asks, scan the bin. Comments are optional.
- A bin with nothing in it is **EMPTY**: scan the bin, never skip it.
- When the bin is finished, **Bin done — nothing more here**. That is what tells the office
  the bin is counted, and it is what moves you on.
- A pallet with no readable label: tap **Counting it as a pallet with no label**; it is
  recorded under the bin and the bin goes on the relabel list. If the number is printed
  and readable, type it instead.
- A pallet the gun says is not on the list: the gun asks; answer honestly. The office sees it
  as an addition.
- A pallet that was on the **Not in Location** list: the gun says *found!* when you scan it.
  Count it as normal; the office is told where it turned up.
- Cannot reach the bin: **Bin blocked — come back later**. Something broken, somebody hurt:
  **SOS** and the reason.

The gun never shows what the system thinks is in the bin. That is deliberate.

## 4 — The Cycle counts page

Sign in on a computer with your login. The sidebar shows **Cycle counts**, the **Testing
Suite** and the **User guide**, and nothing else.

- **Today**: today's batch and how far it has got, by person.
- **Still open**: bins from earlier batches nobody finished.
- **Coverage**: how much of the warehouse has been counted this period.

Your login reads these pages; it does not generate batches or change the programme.

## 5 — Practising

**Testing Suite** in the sidebar puts the real scanner app in a frame with a sheet of test
data beside it; clicking a value "scans" it. *What to practise → Cycle count* sets up a
practice cycle count with a five-bin list: a plain bin, two pallets, an empty bin, a short
pallet, one whose pallet has gone. Nothing you do there touches a real count. Spend twenty
minutes on it before your first morning.

## 6 — When something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle count is not offered at sign-on | No batch today, or the job is switched off for count day | Ask the office |
| Gun: *Offline and no list cached for this session* | The gun has never downloaded this count | Carry it into Wi-Fi once and sign on again |
| Gun shows OFFLINE with a number | Your lines are queued in a dead spot | Nothing; they upload with signal. Do not clear the app |
| *Not signed on — see a supervisor* | Your clock-in number is not on the crew list | See a supervisor; they add you under Teams & crew and you sign on again |
| A keypad covers the screen | The Keyboard button was left on | Tap Keyboard again |
| A scan does nothing | The scanner is not sending ENTER | Tell the office: DataWedge needs setting on that gun |
| Gun: *Bin done — nothing more here* did not move on | Something in the bin is still waiting | Finish the pallet on screen first |
| Gun: *That is a very large quantity* | Out of the ordinary | Type it again if it is right |
| You do not know what a message means | — | Paste it into **User guide → Ask the guide** |
