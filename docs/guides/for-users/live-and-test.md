# LIVE and TEST — how to switch, and tell them apart

**Role:** Anyone who uses the TEST system — supervisors, inventory control, counters  **Login profile:** your own login, or a scanner  **Pages:** every office page, the scanner  **Functions:** switching between LIVE and TEST, knowing which one you are on

There are two copies of the app. **LIVE** is the real count and the real stock. **TEST** is a
copy with its own data, for trying things out — a new setting, a practice count on real guns —
without touching the stock. You switch between them from inside the app, with the same sign-in
and the same gun.

## 1 — In one minute

- **Green ring, LIVE** — the real thing. **Orange ring, TEST** — nothing there is real stock.
- In the office: **Switch to…** at the bottom of the sidebar. On TEST: **Back to LIVE**.
- On a gun: **System: LIVE | TEST** on the sign-on screen.
- You always **sign in on LIVE**. One login works on both.

## 2 — Which one am I on?

| | |
|:--:|:--:|
| ![](../../images/guide-ring-live.png)<br>**LIVE** — a green ring round the logo and name, marked *LIVE* | ![](../../images/guide-ring-test.png)<br>**TEST** — an orange ring marked *TEST*, and an orange bar across the top |

- On the office pages, the logo and site name at the top of the sidebar sit in a **ring of the
  system's color**, with its name on it.
- Every TEST page also has an orange bar: **TEST — not the live system. Nothing counted here is
  the real stock.**
- On a gun, the top of the screen has a **band** of the system's color and the name in a ring.

**Before you count, look at the color.** A real count belongs on LIVE.

## 3 — Switching in the office

- **Signing in:** sign in on LIVE as usual. The sign-in box has **Sign in to: LIVE / TEST** — pick
  TEST to go straight there.
- **To TEST:** **Switch to… → TEST** at the bottom of the sidebar. TEST opens on the same page,
  signed in as you.
- **Back:** **Back to LIVE** on the orange bar, or **Switch to… → LIVE**. You are still signed in.

You only see *Switch to…* if your login has been given TEST.

## 4 — Switching a gun

| | |
|:--:|:--:|
| ![](../../images/guide-gun-system-live.png)<br>**On LIVE**: green, *System: LIVE · TEST* | ![](../../images/guide-gun-system-test.png)<br>**On TEST**: orange, *Back to LIVE* |

1. On the gun's sign-on screen, under **System**, tap **TEST**. The gun moves over under its own
   name; sign on there as usual.
2. To come back, tap **Back to LIVE** on the sign-on screen.
3. A gun with **counts still waiting to send** will not switch until they have gone — they belong
   to the system they were counted in. Get it into Wi-Fi first.

The *System* row only shows when the office lets the guns use both. When the office puts the
scanners on **one system only**, every gun goes there by itself — once its counts have sent —
and has no *System* row: a gun you open on LIVE on a TEST-only day moves to TEST on its own.

## 5 — What to know

- **Nothing mixes.** TEST has its own counts, lists and settings. Counting on TEST never changes
  LIVE.
- **One login.** You never need a separate login or password for TEST.
- **One link per gun.** A gun keeps its own link; it is switched, never set up again.
- **A switched sign-in lasts a shift** on TEST; after that, switch again from LIVE.

## 6 — When something is not right

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No *Switch to…* in the sidebar | Your login has not been given TEST | Ask an admin |
| *this login may not use TEST* | The same | Ask an admin |
| TEST's sign-in box says to sign in on LIVE | You opened TEST directly | Sign in on LIVE, then switch |
| **Back to LIVE** does nothing, or says *this system's own address* | TEST is not pointed at LIVE correctly | Tell an admin; until then, open LIVE's address yourself |
| The gun has no *System* row | The office has the scanners on one system only | Count where it is; ask an admin if that is wrong |
| The gun says *the scanners are on TEST now* and will not sign on | It is going to TEST, and its counts are still sending | Get it into Wi-Fi; it goes by itself |
| *N counts are still on this scanner* | Counts waiting to send | Let them send in Wi-Fi, then switch |
| You counted on TEST by mistake | Those counts are on TEST only | Count the bins again on LIVE; tell your supervisor |
