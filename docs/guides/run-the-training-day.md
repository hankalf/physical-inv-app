# How to — Run the training day

**Role:** Trainers, count supervisors  **Login profile:** Admin (to set the training count up and print), any login with the Testing Suite on the day  **Pages:** Testing Suite → Classroom training, Dashboard → Team plan, Dashboard → Second counts, Reports → Pallet report  **Functions:** the training count, the printed wall, the pocket cards, the films and the slide deck, reset for the next class

This is the trainer's run sheet: what to print and hang the day before, how a two-hour session
goes, what to say at each spot on the wall, what the office screen shows while the class counts,
and how to reset between groups. Everything the class scans is a real count in the app — the
only difference from count day is the name on it: **Training count — not real stock**.

## 2 — The day before

1. **Testing Suite** in the sidebar, as an admin. Under **Classroom training** press **Set up
   the training count**. It makes the count — 18 bins in two short aisles, T01 and T02, with 17
   pallets on its inventory report — plus three training teams (S1-DS01, S1-DS02, S2-DS01) and
   eight trainee badges (TRN-01 to TRN-08), and plans T01 for S1-DS01 and T02 for S1-DS02. It is
   a trial run: nothing on it ever reaches the ERP file, and the scanners see it as **Training
   count — not real stock**. It also makes **Training cycle count — not real stock**: the same
   wall, with a list of six bins for practising cycle counts (section 5a).
2. **🖨 Print the wall (big labels)**. One label per page: rack labels first, then pallet labels,
   then the team cards and badges, then the trainer's key. **🖨 Small labels** puts two to a page
   for a table-top version.
3. **🖨 Pocket cards**, English and Spanish, one card per person. For the real teams, paste the
   team numbers into the box on the card first — one line per person, so a team of three is three
   lines — or press **Fill from Teams & crew**. Every sheet of four fronts is then followed by its
   backs, mirrored: print double-sided, flipped on the long edge, and each person's team card lands
   on the back of their pocket card. Cut along the lines.
4. Open the Dashboard with the training count picked at the top and check **Team plan** shows
   S1-DS01 counting T01 and S1-DS02 counting T02. Nothing else to set.
5. Sign one scanner on yourself — team card S1-DS01, badge TRN-01 — and scan the first two spots
   of the wall, so you have seen it work on this network. Then **Reset for the next class** so
   the class starts clean.

## 4 — The session, minute by minute

About two hours for a group of eight; split a bigger group into two wall sessions.

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Why we count, and the one rule: **count what is really there** | supervisor, 5 min |
| 0:05 | The film, in the room's language, on the big screen | 10 min |
| 0:15 | The deck, scanners in hand, trainer at the front. Stop on every *tip* box | 25 min |
| 0:40 | Hand out the pocket cards. Read the five rules out loud | 5 min |
| 0:45 | **The wall, team one**: S1-DS01 signs on with its card and three badges and counts T01. The rest of the class watches the Dashboard on the big screen | 25 min |
| 1:10 | **The wall, team two**: S1-DS02 counts T02. Team one watches the second counts appear | 15 min |
| 1:25 | The second counts: S2-DS01 signs on and walks the second counts raised by the two short counts | 10 min |
| 1:35 | The office screen: the Pallet report with the class's own mistakes on it, in words. Then the quiz, ten questions, and its answers together | 15 min |
| 1:45 | Questions; everybody signs on once with their own badge; sign off; scanners on the chargers | 15 min |
| +0:20 | **Cycle counts**, for the people who will do them: one trainee at a time works the six-bin list (section 5a) | 20 min |

Let a different person hold the scanner at every spot. The person scanning reads the top line of
the screen out loud before they do anything: that habit is most of the training.

## 5 — The wall run-through, spot by spot

Stand beside the wall with the trainer's key. At each spot, say what the scanner will do before
the trainee does it.

**Sign on.** Team card S1-DS01 into *Team number*, then each badge. Each name shows. **Sign on &
load list**. The scanner says *Your aisle: T01, levels A–B*; **Start counting**. Point at **Next
bin: T01A001** on the gun.

- **T01A001** — the three steps. Rack, beep. Pallet, beep. Count the 40 on the label's line, type
  40, Enter. Skip the comment. *Next bin* moves to T01A002.
- **T01A002** — two pallets. After the first, the box turns orange: *Still in this bin, 1 of 2*.
  Rack again, second pallet, its quantity. Say it: never leave a spot half counted.
- **T01A003** — empty. Rack, then **Bin is EMPTY**, **Yes**. An empty spot that is not recorded
  looks like one nobody reached.
- **T01A004** — the short count. The label says 36; have the class count **30** and type 30. The
  scanner says nothing: it is a blind count. On the Dashboard a second count appears within a
  few seconds. Show it.
- **T01A005** — nothing hangs here. Rack, **Bin is EMPTY**. The report expected TRN-1005: the
  office now sees it MISSING here.
- **T01A006** — TRN-1005 hangs where the report has nothing. Count it as normal. The report shows
  it as WRONG BIN: the office knows exactly where it went.
- **T01A007** — two labels, one of them TRN-9999, which is on no report. After its barcode the
  scanner asks **Count it anyway?** — **YES**, count it. Found stock is never lost.
- **T01A008** — one skid wearing two labels. Rack, then **This pallet has more than one label**,
  then each label with its cases, then **Done**. The rack is scanned once.
- **T01B001** — the torn barcode. Try the trigger twice; it will not read. **Label will not
  scan** → **I can read it — let me type it** → type TRN-1010 → its quantity. The office gets a
  list of labels to reprint.
- **T01B002** — level B, plain. Type 40, then press **Back** once to show a step undone, and type
  40 again. Then show the other fix: tap **History** — only this team's lines, in the last twenty
  bins — and **Void this line** on T01B001. The gun jumps back to the scan screen with **Go back
  to T01B001**: scan its rack and the pallet again. A void never leaves a hole behind.
- **T01B003** — empty, as the report says. Rack, **Bin is EMPTY**, **Yes**.
- **T01B004** — the last spot. After it the scanner says *every bin in T01 has a count — tell your
  supervisor*. There is no button for this on the gun. On the big screen, **Team plan → T01 →
  done**: the scanner's **My aisle** shows the next one.

**Team two** signs on with S1-DS02 and counts T02 the same way. T02A002: count **38** for a
second count. T02A004: after the first pallet, scan the rack of T02A005 by mistake and count TRN-2006 there —
the scanner takes it, and then **Next bin** points back at T02A004, *1 of 2 tags*: the gun sends
you back for the pallet you left. Count TRN-2005 and the spot is whole again. T02A005 is already
done; it is the place to press **SOS — I need help** and pick a reason: on the Dashboard
**Alerts** flashes, a supervisor presses **I am on it**, and the scanner says who has seen it.
T02A006: **Sign off this scanner**.

**The second counts.** S2-DS01 signs on with its card and two badges. **My aisle** shows **Start
second counts**: the two short counts from the wall. At each, count everything in the spot — rack,
pallet, quantity — and the gun moves to the next one by itself once the pallet is counted. The second count agrees with the label, so
the office sees the first count was wrong.

## 5a — Cycle counts on the same wall

A cycle count is one person with a scanner and a short list of bins for the day: each bin is
counted as it stands, then **Bin done**, and the next bin on the list comes up. The wall is the
same; the list is on its own count, **Training cycle count — not real stock**. The trainer's key has
it at the back, under *Cycle count practice*. Play the cycle-count film first
(`scanner-training-cycle-en.mp4` or `-es.mp4`).

**Sign on.** At the top of the sign-on screen tap **Cycle count**, and pick the training cycle
count. There is no team card on a cycle count: the person is the team, so **one badge** only —
TRN-07, say. **Sign on & load list**, then **Start counting the list**. The banner says
*CYCLE COUNT · bin T01A002*.

| # | Bin | On the wall | Say at this bin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | T01A002 | TRN-1002, TRN-1003 | Two pallets: rack, pallet, quantity for each, then **Bin done**. The next bin comes up |
| 2 | T01A003 | nothing | Rack label, **Bin is EMPTY**, **Yes**: an empty bin finishes by itself |
| 3 | T01A007 | TRN-1007, TRN-9999 | TRN-9999 asks *Count it anyway?* — **YES**. Then **Bin done** |
| 4 | T01B001 | TRN-1010, torn | **Label will not scan** → **I can read it — let me type it** → TRN-1010 on the scanner's keys |
| 5 | T02A004 | TRN-2004, TRN-2005 | Scan T02A005's rack first, on purpose: *This cycle count is for bin T02A004*. Then both pallets, **Bin done** |
| 6 | T02A006 | TRN-2007 | The last one: **Bin done**, and the list is empty. **Sign off this scanner**; the next trainee signs on |

On the office side, **Cycle Counts** in the sidebar, with the training cycle count picked, shows the
list going down bin by bin, who did each and when. **Reset for the next class** clears the cycle
count too and puts the six bins back on the list.

## 6 — What the office screen shows

Keep the Dashboard on the big screen with the training count picked, and switch tabs as the class
goes:

- **Team plan** while they sign on: S1-DS01 goes from *waiting* to *counting now* in T01, and the
  bar fills as the spots are counted.
- **Second counts** after T01A004: the short count arrives as an open second count for any team
  but S1-DS01.
- **Alerts** at the SOS.
- **Reports → Pallet report** at the end: TRN-1004 **QTY VARIANCE**, TRN-1005 **WRONG BIN**,
  TRN-9999 **NOT IN MASTER**, TRN-1010 in the labels-to-fix list, and the empties recorded. The
  class sees their own morning in the words the office uses.

The report's Cost columns are there too; the point to make is that **the scanner never showed a
cost or an expected quantity**.

## 7 — The roster: who has been trained

Under the buttons on the Classroom training card is the **Training roster**: every employee
uploaded on Teams & crew (the wall's eight trainees are not on it), with their shift, team and
department. As each person finishes the wall, tick their box. The app keeps who ticked them and
when, and the summary at the top counts the trained by shift, so at the end of the day you can see
who still needs a session.

- **On the wall** fills in by itself when a person's own badge signs on to the training count, as a
  hint; the tick is still yours to give.
- **Not yet trained only**, the shift box and the search narrow the list for a big crew.
- **🖨 Print the roster** is the clipboard version, a sheet per shift with a box to tick and a line
  to sign. **⬇ roster.csv** is the same list for HR or the supervisors.
- A tick can be taken back. Nothing on the roster touches the count itself.

## 8 — Between classes, and after

- **Reset for the next class** clears every count, sign-on, second count and alert on the training
  count and puts the aisles back at the start. The wall stays as it is. Press it between groups
  and at the end of the day.
- **Remove the training count** takes the count, its teams and its trainee badges away. Do this
  before count day, so the training teams are not on the Crew board and TRN badges cannot sign on.
- **Set up the training count** again next time: everything comes back the same, so the printed
  wall is still right.
- On count day the real teams sign on with **their** team cards and badges. The pocket card is
  the same.

## 9 — Checklist

- ☐ Training count set up; **Team plan** shows T01 and T02 planned
- ☐ The training cycle count shows six bins on its list (the Classroom training card says so)
- ☐ Wall printed and hung in the key's order; TRN-1005 under T01A006, TRN-9999 beside TRN-1007
- ☐ Team cards and badges on the sign-on table
- ☐ Pocket cards cut, in both languages
- ☐ Film and deck open on the big screen in the room's language; Dashboard in a second tab
- ☐ Scanners charged, on Wi-Fi, at the sign-on screen; one spot scanned yourself the day before
- ☐ **Reset for the next class** pressed before the class walks in
- ☐ Roster ticked for everyone who finished; **⬇ roster.csv** sent to the supervisors
- ☐ After the last group: **Reset**, and **Remove** before count day
