# How to — Run the floor on count day

**Role:** Floor supervisors and leads  **Login profile:** Supervisor or Admin  **Pages:** Dashboard → Team plan, Alerts, Second counts, Reports; the Crew board  **Functions:** what to watch while the teams count, and what to press

One page for the supervisor on the floor. The counters have their pocket cards and *On the
scanner*; this is the other side of it: where each thing the scanners do shows up in the office,
and the button that answers it. Keep the Dashboard open with the count picked at the top.

## Before the first team

- **Dashboard**, the count picked at the top. **Team plan** shows each team's first aisle as
  *counting now* or *next*; **Crew board** (top right of the Dashboard) goes on the floor TV.
- Every scanner charged, on the Wi-Fi, at the sign-on screen. Each crew has its **team card** and
  their **badges**.
- Settings → Count setup → **Guided by the plan, bin by bin** is on, and **Show on the scanners**.

## While the teams count

| On the floor | Where you see it | What you press |
|---|---|---|
| A team says *every bin has a count* | **Team plan**: the aisle's bar is full | **done** on that aisle. Their next aisle comes up on their scanner by itself |
| A team is waiting on racking another team is in | **Team plan**: *Held · team …* | Nothing: it starts when the racking is free. **start** to send them in anyway |
| **SOS** on a scanner | **Alerts**: a loud alert with team, aisle and last bin | **I am on it** — the scanner says who has seen it — and go |
| A scanner has gone quiet | **Alerts**: the team, and how long | **On break**, **Lunch**, or **Seen — I am on it** |
| You need a team somewhere | **Alerts → Message the floor** | Pick a team or everybody, type it, send. You see when they tap *Got it* |
| A first count disagrees with the report | **Second counts**: raised by itself, for another team | Nothing: teams pick them up from **Start second counts** |
| You want a spot counted again | **Second counts**: type the bin | **Request second count**, with a team if you want one |
| A torn label, no label, a rack label that will not scan | **Reports → Fix list**: *Labels to replace* | Print one, put it on, then **Label printed & on the pallet** (or **on the rack**) |
| Damage, a blocked spot | **Reports → Fix list**: *Damage*, *Blocked bins to go back to* | Sort it, then **Fixed** |
| A pallet not on the list, in the wrong bin, missing | **Reports → Pallet report**: *NOT IN MASTER*, *WRONG BIN*, *MISSING* | Nothing on the floor: second counts and adjustments take it from there |

## At the end of a shift

- Every crew signs off on its scanner: **Sign off this scanner**, and the scanner on its charger.
  A scanner with lines still queued warns before it signs off — walk it to the Wi-Fi.
- **Team plan**: every aisle counted is marked **done**; anything half done stays queued for the
  next shift.
- **Second counts**: the open number. Hand what is left to the next shift's second-count team.
- **Reports → Fix list**: labels still to replace, so the next shift's counters are not stopped by
  the same torn label.

## Do not

- Do not mark an aisle **done** before the team says every bin has a count: the expected pallets
  nobody saw become second counts.
- Do not tell a counter what the first count was on a second count. It is blind on purpose.
- Do not let a crew carry on as another team's number. The next crew signs on with its own card.
